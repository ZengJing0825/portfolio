One AI investment-research agent product, six steps from defining the output to acquiring users, all built by me; every step has code or rules you can check.
1Define the output→2Build the agent→3Evaluate→4Push to users→5Guard quality→6Acquire and attribute↻ step 6 feeds back into step 1
1

Define the output: what the agent says and does not say

Without a defined form: walls of narration, the same name repeated, no conclusion up front.

Five output templates: digest, screener, thesis, what-if, earnings

Three rules: conclusion in the first fold; one page answers one question; a digest is a push, not a dashboard

Three grounding gates and a frozen feed contract written into the templates, so rules are checkable

4 templates (11,403 lines) adopted into the team library; 3 PRs to a public repo, 2 merged the same day

2

Build the agent: make the rules run every day

Holdings and watchlists drown users; they only want to know whether anything happened and what it means.

Rank by event, not by ticker; a move without its own news triggers a peer and sector look-up

Every line carries a 'what it means, what to watch' lens

Positions by manual entry, screenshot or broker connection; engine reused for a watchlist sibling in two days

~14K lines of automation, 5 design docs: portfolio-watch, watchlist-watch

3

Evaluate: know before releaseopen source

Prompt changes judged by feel; releases without a regression list.

Get questions and answers right first: owner writes, peer reviews

Four tiers with targets; versioned judges calibrated by human audit

Categorise bad cases (data, tool, ambiguity, reasoning, judge) before feeding back

Set 30 → 100; first pass 5/27, above 0.9 three months later; 145 tests: agent-eval-harness

4

Push to users: quiet unless something happenedopen source

A late push is worthless; a noisy one gets muted.

No push without an event; re-push only on escalation

One signal set sliced by ticker, person or book; position-aware ranking by weight × move

Propose, approve, publish; an audit record per run

Prototype: 16 pushes against 3 quiet runs in a day, near-duplicates within the hour, until a minimum interval fixed it; 61 tests: ticker-digest

5

Guard quality: keep bad content and data off the homepage

User-generated content flooded in; nobody can watch the homepage by hand.

Scoring rule: gates first, universal plus skill dimensions, single attribution, grade bands

Hourly health check with 10 layers that tell 'empty' from 'broken', baseline and alerts

44 items scored, 19 featured, mean 70.1: scoring rubric, curation SOP, monitor script

6

Acquire and attribute: every ad dollar traceableopen source

Money spent without knowing where users went; algorithms trained on the wrong goal.

Track before you spend; reconcile GA4 against first-party data

Split by landing type, not totals

Every change: diagnose, hypothesis, small budget, compare, kill or scale

Cost per signup down to about a third in three months; use-case pages cheapest and steadiest, roughly 2x the CTR of PMax: ga4-ads-dashboard

All numbers are my own records. The three open-source items are at github.com/ZengJing0825; the rest can be shown in an interview.
